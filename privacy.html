Privacy Policy

Arasandoral Uploader · Last updated November 6, 2026

Who uses this app

Arasandoral Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the "Into the Gates of Arasandoral" YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel, set those videos' thumbnails, and add them to a playlist on the same channel. It does not read, collect or process data from any other person's account.

How data is stored

The only Google user data the app keeps is the OAuth credential needed to act on the owner's channel. It is stored as a private secret in the owner's own automation environment and is never published, sold or shared with anyone.

Limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
