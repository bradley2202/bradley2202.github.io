Arasandoral Uploader

A personal publishing tool for the "Into the Gates of Arasandoral" YouTube channel.

Arasandoral Uploader is a private automation used only by the owner of the "Into the Gates of Arasandoral" YouTube channel. It assembles long-form fantasy and sci-fi music videos from Creative Commons–licensed music and original artwork, then publishes them to that channel.

Through Google's OAuth, it uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel,
  • set each video's custom thumbnail, and
  • add uploaded videos to a playlist on that channel.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
